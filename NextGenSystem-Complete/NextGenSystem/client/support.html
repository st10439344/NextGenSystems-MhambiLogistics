HELP CENTRE

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to some of the most common questions from our logistics clients.

Where can I track my shipment?

Open the Track Shipment section from your Client Portal and enter your shipment reference number to view its current status.

How do I request a quotation?

Select Request Quote from the Client Portal navigation and complete the logistics quotation form with your transport requirements.

Where are my documents?

Your available invoices, delivery documents and other logistics records can be accessed from the Documents section.

Can I update my company details?

Yes. Open Company Profile from the Client Portal and use the Edit Profile option to update your registered information.
