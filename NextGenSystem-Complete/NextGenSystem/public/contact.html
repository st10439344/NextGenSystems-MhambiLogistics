FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How Can We Help?

Below are answers to some of the questions our customers ask most frequently.

How long does it take to receive a quotation?

Our Procurement Department aims to prepare and respond to quotation requests within 24 business hours. Complex logistics projects may require additional assessment.

Can I track my shipment online?

Yes. Once your shipment has been approved and scheduled, you will receive a shipment reference number that can be used on our Track Shipment page.

Which provinces do you deliver to?

Mhambi Logistics provides transportation and logistics solutions across all nine provinces of South Africa.

Do you provide warehousing services?

Yes. We provide secure warehousing, inventory management and distribution services for businesses requiring short-term or long-term storage.
