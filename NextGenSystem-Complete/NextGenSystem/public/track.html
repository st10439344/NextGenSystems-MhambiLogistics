FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Shipment Tracking FAQs

Answers to some of the most common questions about shipment tracking and delivery.

How often is my shipment updated?

Shipment information is updated automatically whenever a driver reaches a checkpoint, collection point, warehouse or delivery destination.

What happens if my shipment is delayed?

If an unexpected delay occurs, the system updates your shipment status automatically and our logistics team will contact you where necessary.

Can I change my delivery address?

Delivery changes can be requested before the shipment reaches the final delivery stage. Please contact Customer Support immediately.

What if I lose my tracking number?

Please contact Mhambi Logistics Customer Support with your quotation reference, company details or collection information and our team will assist you.
