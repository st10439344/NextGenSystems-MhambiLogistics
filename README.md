# Mhambi Logistics Enterprise Logistics Management Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Mhambi Logistics Enterprise Logistics Management Platform** is a web-based logistics management solution designed to support the operations of a professional logistics and transportation company.

The platform provides a centralised digital environment where **clients and internal logistics departments** can access services and manage logistics-related activities.

The system is being developed by **NextGen Systems** as part of the Mhambi Logistics project.

---

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The purpose of the Mhambi Logistics platform is to improve the way logistics information, customer interactions and internal operational activities are managed through a centralised digital system.

The platform aims to improve:

* Customer communication
* Shipment visibility
* Quotation requests
* Document accessibility
* Logistics coordination
* Internal departmental workflows
* Information management
* Operational efficiency

---

## 🌐 Platform Structure

The complete platform is designed around two main areas:

### Public Website

The public-facing website allows visitors and potential clients to learn more about Mhambi Logistics and its services.

It includes:

* Home
* About
* Services
* Fleet
* Industries
* Careers
* Contact
* Client Login
* Client Registration
* Quote Access
* Shipment Tracking

### Client Portal

Registered business clients can access a dedicated portal containing:

* Client Dashboard
* Request a Quote
* Shipment Tracking
* Shipment History
* Documents
* Company Profile
* Support
* Logout

### Internal Logistics Management System

The internal system is structured around different organisational roles:

#### Administrator

Responsible for:

* User Management
* Role Management
* Company Management
* System Settings
* Audit Logs
* Data Management
* Reports and Analytics
* Notifications
* Profile Management

#### Dispatcher

Responsible for:

* Shipment Creation
* Driver Assignment
* Route Planning
* Shipment Tracking
* Delivery Scheduling
* Shipment Reports

#### Warehouse

Responsible for:

* Inventory
* Stock Management
* Receiving Stock
* Dispatching Stock
* Stock Transfers
* Low-Stock Alerts
* Inventory Reports

#### Fleet

Responsible for:

* Vehicle Management
* Vehicle Maintenance
* Fuel Management
* Vehicle Assignments
* Fleet Tracking
* Maintenance Scheduling
* Fleet Reports

#### Procurement

Responsible for:

* Supplier Management
* Purchase Requests
* Purchase Orders
* Goods Received
* Supplier Invoices
* Procurement Reports

#### Finance

Responsible for:

* Invoices
* Payments
* Receipts
* Accounts Payable
* Accounts Receivable
* Expenses
* Budgets
* Financial Reports

#### Driver

Responsible for:

* Assigned Shipments
* Delivery Schedule
* Navigation
* Proof of Delivery
* Delivery History
* Incident Reporting
